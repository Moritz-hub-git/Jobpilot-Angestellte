Dear SIXT Hiring Team,

Scaling AI from proof of concept to real business impact is what I do today. Why SIXT: because you are serious about agentic AI at enterprise scale, and ONE — with 350,000 vehicles and millions of drivers — is an operations landscape where deployed agents translate directly into measurable value. I also work best at real pace: my principles are structured data before intelligence; automate the process, not just the output; build fast, iterate, deliver; keep the human in the loop by design. My notice period is three months. I would be glad to walk you through the artifacts behind every claim in this letter — the agents, the platform, the numbers.
